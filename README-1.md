# GROW ALERT 

### **Description:** 

A light-based plant growing system that supports plant growth and supports an alarm when sufficient light is unavailable. 

## **Required Materials** : 

1. Colour A4 Sheet 

2. Plant 

3. Glitter Tape and Foam Sheet 

4. 9V Battery 

5. Bread Board 

6. Connecting Wires 

7. Buzzer 

8. Resistors 

9. NPN Transistor 

- 10.Cardboard 

### **Procedure:** 

1. Fix the LDR and LED Light near the plant growing setup. 

2. Connect the LDR, resistors and Transistor on the Breadboard. 

3. Connect the LED and Buzzer to the transistor circuit. 

4. Connect the circuit to the 9V battery. 

5. When sufficient light falls on the sensor, the circuit remains in normal condition. 

6. When the light is blocked or becomes insufficient, the transistor switches ON and the buzzer sounds like an alert. 

7. Test the setup by covering and uncovering sensor. 

### **Notes:** 

The system uses light intensity to monitor the plant growing environment. When the sufficient light is available, the plant can receive the requirement light. When the light is blocked or becomes insufficient, the buzzer gives an alert. 

The circuit works using a battery, Sensor, transistor and Buzzer. 

Keep the circuit connections tight and Correct.
